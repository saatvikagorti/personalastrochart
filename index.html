How KP Works
Sign Lord → Star Lord → Sub Lord

Krishnamurti Paddhati refines the sidereal zodiac to a much finer resolution. Every point in the sky has a Sign Lord (ruler of the rasi), a Star Lord (ruler of the nakshatra), and — KP's signature contribution — a Sub Lord, a subdivision of the nakshatra sized proportionally to that planet's Vimshottari dasha years. The Sub Lord is what "pinpoints" whether and how a matter will materialize, and through which houses.

Sub Lord: Moon

Moon owns your 10th house in this chart, so your overall life direction (1st) is closely bound up with career and public standing (10th) — who you are and what you're known for tend to move together.

10th Cusp — Career
Sub Lord: Jupiter

Jupiter owns your 3rd (skill, communication, effort) and 6th (service, competition, daily work) houses — career growth here tends to run through skill-building, communication, and consistent, service-oriented effort rather than sudden breaks.

7th Cusp — Partnerships
Sub Lord: Rahu

Rahu is a node rather than a house owner, which in KP often points to partnerships that feel karmically charged, unconventional, or evolve in unexpected directions rather than following a fixed script.

2nd & 11th Cusps — Finances
Sub Lords: Mercury & Mars

Mercury (2nd, owns 9th & 12th) links resources to learning and less-visible income streams; Mars (11th, owns 2nd & 7th) links gains to direct action and partnerships — money matters here tend to move through both intellect and initiative.

Why Navamsa
The D9 — Marriage, Dharma & Inner Strength

The Navamsa splits every sign into nine 3°20' slices and re-maps them across the zodiac. It's traditionally read as the chart's "fine print" — used to judge the true strength of a planet, and especially to look at marriage, dharma, and long-term inner resilience.

Ashtakoot Guna Milan
The 36-Point Vedic Matching System

This is the traditional koota-based compatibility check used before Vedic/Telugu marriages — it compares the Moon's Nakshatra and Rashi for each person across 8 factors (varna, vashya, tara, yoni, graha maitri, gana, bhakoot, nadi) for a score out of 36. Only date, time, and UTC offset are needed — Moon position doesn't meaningfully depend on birth location.

Ashtakoot is the widely-used 8-factor system common across most of India.
